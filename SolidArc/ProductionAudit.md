## 3. Root-Cause Analysis & Fixes for Slow / Freezing Booleans

### 3.1 Root Causes Identified During Audit (`⏱️` / `🐞`)
- **🐞 Analytic-to-NURBS promotion in Boolean Cutter Library (`BodyTransform.cpp`):** Placing and stretching a KitOps/library cutter (`Round`, `Slot`, `Box`, `Gusset`) applies a non-uniform diagonal scale in the cutter's local frame (`ScaleX ≠ ScaleY` or `ScaleZ ≠ ScaleX`). Previously, `TransformBody` promoted every non-uniformly scaled `CylinderSurface` or `ConeSurface` into a rational tensor-product `NurbsSurface` when `ScaleX ≠ ScaleY`, even when the scale along the cylinder's two radial axes was isotropic and only the axial height stretched (`AxialScale ≠ RadialScale`). Intersecting two promoted rational NURBS surfaces triggered expensive subdivision + marching SSI instead of closed-form quadric SSI.
- **⏱️ Uncached O(N × M) ray-mesh rebuilding in `GeneralFuse` (`WindingNumber.cpp`, `GeneralFuse.cpp`):** Classifying `K` candidate cells rebuilt full face triangulations for `BodyA` and `BodyB` on every `ClassifyPointInSolid` query and always cast all 5 rays even when the first 3 rays agreed unanimously.
- **⏱️ Missing disjoint AABB fast paths in `GeneralFuse` (`GeneralFuse.cpp`):** Moving a Live Boolean cutter outside the target's bounding box still executed full cell decomposition, boundary paving, and winding classification instead of returning an instant clone (`Subtract`), empty result (`Intersect`), or multi-region assembly (`Union`).
- **⏱️ Untrimmed NURBS carrier marching in `SurfaceIntersection` (`SurfaceIntersection.cpp`):** Face-pair SSI marched across full untrimmed carrier domains even when one face's active UV loop bounding box was disjoint from the partner surface's 3D AABB.
- **⏱️ Uncached STEP imports & scene re-tessellation (`FeatureTree.cpp`, `SolidArcScene.cpp`):** Replaying trees containing `ImportStep` re-parsed STEP text on every regeneration, and `BuildSolidArcScene` re-tessellated unchanged bodies when sibling cutters moved.
- **🐞 `StageGizmoPreview` throttle timestamp bug (`SolidArcInterface.cpp`):** `Gizmo.QuietSince` was overwritten on every mouse-delta frame rather than on preview completion, starving continuous gizmo drags on heavy (>50 ms) compound stacks.

### 3.2 Measured Performance Results (`W72` & `W1`–`W78`)
- **Worst-case Boolean Library cut + full viewport scene build (`W72`):** **14.9–15.7 ms** across `Box`, `Round`, `Slot`, and `Hex` library cutters (gate budget `≤ 50 ms` — **60+ FPS interactive**).
- **Analytic preservation (`W72`):** Coaxial stretched `Round` cutter produces **0 NURBS faces** (exact `CylinderSurface` + `PlaneSurface` preserved).
- **Preview document clone (`W31`):** **112.8× faster** than full replay (`ModifierCheckpoint` + `RegenerationCache` + `StepImportCache`).
- **Full `W1`–`W30` regression header:** **37.51 s** (faster than **39.44 s** baseline despite richer modifier/parameter digesting).

### 4.4 Viewport Gizmo Fixes (Top/Cardinal Views & Curve Handles) and Continued / Shared-Endpoint V-Lofts (`W81`–`W82`)
- **🐞 Top & Cardinal 90° View Gizmo Single-Axis Defect (`SolidArcInterface.cpp`):** Previously, `FitGizmoToView` only re-oriented `Bodies`, `Shape` (vertices/edges), and `Faces` to World `{X, Y, Z}` when `Gizmo.Pose.Axes[2]` was not perpendicular to the camera ray; `Sketches` and `Points` on `XZ`/`YZ` planes kept their local sketch axes (`Axes[0] = X`, `Axes[2] = ∓Y`), hiding `Axes[0]` in Front/Back/Right/Left or hiding `Axes[1]` (`Z`) in Top/Bottom so **only 1 green/red axis appeared instead of 2 screen-plane controls**. In addition, `ResolveGizmoTarget` returned early in Mode `1` (`ControlPoint`) whenever `SketchPoints.Sketch` was empty, hiding gizmos for selected curves, sketches, and bodies in Mode `1`, and `SpaceCurve` steps ignored transform (`G`/`R`/`S`) and duplicate operations (`SolidArcDocument.cpp`).
  - **Fix:** `FitGizmoToView` now aligns **all** non-facing targets (`Bodies`, `Shape`, `Faces`, `Sketches`, `Points`, and `Poles`) to World `{X, Y, Z}` in every 90° cardinal view (`Top`, `Bottom`, `Front`, `Back`, `Right`, `Left`), showing both screen-plane arrows and the screen-plane quad (`2` arrows + `1` quad in every 90° view; `3` arrows + `3` quads in 3D). Added `SolidArcGizmoTarget::Poles` + `MoveSolidArcPoles` so clicking curve handles on **both** 2D sketches and 3D `SpaceCurve`s in **both** Mode `1` (`ControlPoint`) and Mode `5` (`Any`) displays and drags a full gizmo, plus full `SpaceCurve` transform/duplicate support. Verified across **1,058 / 1,058** combinations in `W81`.
- **🐞 Continuing Lofts & Shared-Endpoint V-Lofts (`SectionLoft.{h,cpp}`, `PanelAssembly.cpp`, `FeatureTree.cpp`, `CurveNetwork.cpp`, `SolidArcDocument.{h,cpp}`, `SolidArcInterface.cpp`):** Continuing a 2-curve loft by adding a 3rd curve at an endpoint of one curve in a V (including at a different height) previously failed across five independent layers:
  1. `OrientSections` (`SectionLoft.cpp`) aligned sections by dot product (`Dot < 0`), flipping obtuse (`> 90°`) V-curves so their shared corner tore apart instead of hinging.
  2. `AssemblePanels` (`PanelAssembly.cpp`) rejected any loft panel with a collapsed `u`-rail (`LeftCollapsed || RightCollapsed`), refusing every shared-endpoint V-loft with `"collapsed boundary (degenerate corners)"`.
  3. Closing a 3-panel tube around `> 90°` dihedral angles could orient the 3rd panel with a Möbius half-twist (`"one-sided"`), and a 3-way T-fin branching off an already-sewn interior seam failed whole-body sewing.
  4. `DetectCurveNetwork` (`CurveNetwork.cpp`) routed any 3 touching curves into `ConstructNetworkSurface`, which immediately refused `< 4` curves (`needs at least 4 curves (2 + 2), got 3`).
  5. Consumed curves of `Loft 1` were hidden into `RemovedCurves`, while `Loft` in the UI neither accepted laminar B-Rep edges of `Loft 1` + `Curve 3` nor `SheetBody` + `Curve 3`.
  - **Fix:** (a) Endpoint-coincidence priority in `OrientSections` keeps shared V-corners paired across all acute and obtuse angles (`0°–180°`); (b) `AssemblePanels` and `LoftSectionSheet` transpose any collapsed `u`-rail onto a singular `v`-row (`dv = 0`) and omit zero-length rows from the fin loop (supporting 1-corner V-fins and 2-corner lens lofts); (c) `FeatureTree` untwists 2-section Möbius loop closures, falls back to standalone sheets on 3-way T-fins, decomposes 3-section open V-hinged lofts into sewn 2-panel chains without 180° hairpin folds, and solves 3-curve closed triangular loops via exact singular Coons patches (`LoftTriangularLoop`); (d) `DetectCurveNetwork` requires `>= 4` curves; and (e) `LoftSolidArcSelection` + UI operations support continuing lofts directly from `Curve + Curve`, `Laminar Edge + Curve`, or `Sheet Body + Curve` (preserving `RemovedCurves ⊂ HiddenCurves` TOML invariant). Verified across all geometries in `W82`.
