What the proofs measure

Computed from the actual fitted B-spline pole nets, not AI-generated images. The browser shows sampled triangles for display; the downloads contain real NURBS surfaces.

Deviation colouring measures CAD grid points to the nearest triangle of the level-3 limit-position reference. The inspector reports CAD-grid-to-source distances for the displayed revision. Both revisions use conforming shared-edge tessellation and analytic normals. The change map measures the refined sample to the previous CAD region (a union of old faces for merged strips), not same-UV displacement. The roof insert clearance move is an actual rigid CAD translation, not a visibility trick. Fairing intentionally departs from polygon noise; a warmer source-distance map is not evidence of worse smoothness. Neither is a certified global Hausdorff bound.

No wheels, interior, aero extras, thickness or underside are included. Zebra is a screening view, not a G1/G2 continuity certificate.
