What the proofs measure

Computed from the actual fitted B-spline pole nets, not AI-generated images. The browser shows sampled triangles for display; the downloads contain real NURBS surfaces.

Deviation colouring measures CAD grid points to the nearest triangle of the level-3 limit-position reference. The inspector reports CAD-grid-to-source distances for the displayed revision. The change map measures the faired sample to the nearest triangle of its original CAD patch, not same-UV displacement. Fairing intentionally departs from polygon noise; a warmer source-distance map is not evidence of worse smoothness. Neither is a certified global Hausdorff bound.
