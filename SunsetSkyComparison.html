🔬 Physical Atmosphere & Shader Improvements Made

The compute shader Engine/SlateVulkan/Device/AtmospherePresentationSurface/Shader/DynamicSkySurface.slang has been re-architected with physical optics equations:

1. Golden-Orange Horizon & Azure Zenith

Uses Rayleigh wavelength extinction coefficients ($\lambda_R = [0.18, 0.42, 1.0]$) and Chappuis ozone absorption. At low sun angles ($0^\circ ext{ to } 10^\circ$), Rayleigh scatters away blue/green along long solar paths, producing a fiery golden-orange horizon glow while ozone keeps the zenith deep azure blue.

2. Spherical Planet Optical Airmass Geometry

Replaced flat planar airmass 1 / (view.y + 0.13) with spherical atmosphere chord length 1 / (viewCos + 0.045 * exp(-viewCos * 7.5)). Eliminates midday horizon fog and ensures noon looks crisp and clear.

3. Smooth Twilight & Pre-Dawn Transitions

As the sun dips below $0^\circ$, civil twilight ($0^\circ ext{ to } -6^\circ$) transitions into crimson/navy, nautical twilight ($-6^\circ ext{ to } -12^\circ$) fades into bluish-blackish horizon glow, and astronomical twilight ($< -12^\circ$) completes into a dark night sky. Completely eliminates the white horizon line artifact!
