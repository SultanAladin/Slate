WHAT THIS IS ▾

A WebGPU port of the Unreal Engine Lumen-style diffuse GI architecture, rebuilt from scratch — no code from the C++ distance-field path is used:

1. Every surface lives once in a surface cache: six orthographic cards per primitive in one atlas holding albedo, normal and emissive.

2. All primitives are composed into one global distance field — a single 3D texture. Every trace in the frame (GI candidates, sun shadow rays, cache radiosity rays, the debug raymarch) sphere-traces that texture and nothing else; no shader trace loops over objects. The voxelisation pass is the only place primitives are enumerated, exactly like Unreal composing mesh fields into its global field.

3. The cache is lit by direct sun (field-traced shadows) plus one stochastic radiosity gather per texel per frame, fed back through the previous frame's lit cache — so bounce accumulates over frames into multi-bounce.

4. Screen-space GI is ReSTIR GI: two cosine candidates per pixel are field-traced, shaded from the surface cache at the hit, then merged through temporal and spatial reservoir resampling with target-function re-evaluation, an RIS weight W = Σw / (M · p̂), a clamped Jacobian for spatial reuse, confidence caps and a final visibility trace of the winning sample.

The car is the Project Drive Control Vehicle: the authored body mesh (1,492 verts / 2,924 tris, per-face material families from the .blend) with procedural spinning/steering wheels at the authored axle sockets (wheelbase 3.396 m, tyre radius 0.50855 m), driven by a bicycle model. Its distance-field primitives and surface-cache cards move with it, so the global field is rebuilt around it every frame.
