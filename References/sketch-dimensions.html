How to use

  1. Drag off an edge to pull out a linear dimension.
  2. Drag off a circle for ⌀ diameter — hold Alt for R radius.
  3. Drag off a fillet arc for its R radius.
  4. Double-click any dimension value to type a new one — geometry updates as you type.
  5. Drag an existing dimension to reposition it.
  6. Click a green widget to toggle that constraint.
  7. Wheel zoom at cursor · Middle/Space pan · Del remove dim
